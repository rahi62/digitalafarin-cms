# Community and Commercial Product Boundary

The public repository is the **Community Edition** and is licensed under Apache-2.0.

The intended commercial model is open-core: the Community Edition should stay genuinely useful, while hosted or advanced capabilities can be offered separately.

Potential commercial products include:

- DigitalAfarin CMS Cloud / managed hosting
- Advanced technical SEO crawler and scheduled audits
- Search Console intelligence and content-decay monitoring
- AI SEO assistant and usage credits
- Advanced internal-link automation
- Agency workspace, quotas and centralized client management
- White-label administration
- Enterprise SSO, SLA, support and custom integrations
- Migration and implementation services

Commercial code should live in separate private packages/repositories so the licensing boundary remains clear.
